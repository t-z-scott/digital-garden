> *an evolving document on how my system works. i had these as separate notes in capacities, but i didn't get a clear overview that way. headings are steps in the creativity flywheel.* 

# capture 📥

- all notes, tasks, ideas, and decisions come in through Obsidian's daily note. i review this every evening.
	- inspired by [My Simple Productivity System (for normal people)!](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7M6bIeVbCqA) 
- all articles that interest me are saved to Readwise Reader. i have a shortlist with a maximum of twenty to stop it from being overwhelming.

# curate 🫳🏾

the notes that still inspire me to create something at the end-of-day shutdown after they were captured will be processed by type.

>[!summary] summary
>- idea, task, decision, experiment project, productivity/system article -> create note in obsidian
>- other article -> link in Capacities daily note
>- \>5 min task -> do it or delegate


# cultivate 🪴

>[!idea] idea
> go in with an opinion and have the reading change my mind.

i plan to go through all the sources and make notes by the end of the next day. (this is to stop me from passively consuming / bed-rotting and to keep the information fresh in my mind.) if those ideas match an idea i already have, i gather those notes and add to an opinion note or web weave. i might braindump about the topic in that created note as well.


# connect 🖇️

i write the mini essay or blog post in either Cap or Obsidian (wherever i made the original notes), following the outline to make sure i don't get off-topic.


# create 🎨

i post it wherever it needs to go, and keep it in my notes to refer back to.
