> *context: This is a reminder to myself.* 

>[!quote]
> "The trick to writing is to stop not writing."
> –Me

1. Address your fears, then do it anyway. [[Do it messy, do it scared, do it unprepared\|Do it messy, do it scared, do it unprepared]].
2. List what you've learned, keep it updated, and write what you're most excited about. Read other blog posts and write about what they did well. Basically, **take notes**.
3. **Write for an audience** (this audience can be Past or Future You).
4. **Build an outline** to organize your thoughts. I suggest having these as a template in the same place that you're taking notes.
5. If you're stuck, **do an activity away from the computer.** Rizel's tip of *taking a shower* really does work, and so does *mental or physical exercise*.
6. **Get rid of distractions.** Do what helps you focus.
7. **Write how you talk.** Literally. Record your voice.
8. **Read your writing out loud** or use a text reader.
9. Share a draft with someone to get feedback (or post and wait for feedback to come to you).
10. If you want better numbers/viewership/SEO, add a cover image and share the published link on social media.
