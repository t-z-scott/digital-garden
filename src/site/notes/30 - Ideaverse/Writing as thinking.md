>[!quote] 
> "I don't know what I think until I write it."
> –unknown

This is so true. I've never been more in touch with myself, my thoughts, and my ideas than I am when I'm writing them down.

[[30 - Ideaverse/Freewriting\|Freewriting]] works because ***writing makes your thoughts visible***, and once you can see them, you can move them, discard them, work with them, whatever the case may be.
