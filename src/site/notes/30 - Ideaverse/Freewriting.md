An exercise I do when overwhelmed:

1. Grab my notebook and a pen.
2. Write everything I'm thinking about until I feel better.
3. Take a short break.
4. Go back over what I wrote and analyze.

This is ***freewriting***. Not doing this means my brain will ruminate, circulating the same thoughts over and over until I close the loop. It works because [[30 - Ideaverse/Writing as thinking\|writing makes your thoughts visible]].
